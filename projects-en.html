PAT Planalto Sul

  • Santa Catarina
  • frogs
  • Cycloramphus valae

Technical support for the analysis of audio data collected in the southern region of Santa Catarina by developing custom classification models. The models assist in the search for a rare frog species not observed in the region for decades (Cycloramphus valae).

Instituto Fauna Brasil

  • Florianópolis-SC
  • primates
  • Alouatta guariba

Technical support for the analysis of audio data collected in Florianópolis, Santa Catarina, by developing custom classification models to support the monitoring of the brown howler monkey (Alouatta guariba) following its reintroduction process.

Ecos da Escuridão - CECAV

  • Bahia
  • caves
  • ecoacoustics

Research project investigating the use of acoustic monitoring as a tool to complement active searches and expand knowledge of cave biodiversity in northeastern Bahia, integrating bioacoustic and ecoacoustic approaches with taxonomic inventories. The project aims to evaluate the relationship between acoustic and taxonomic diversity, identify cave-specific acoustic signatures, and document new species and sound repertoires, thereby contributing to the advancement of subterranean ecology.
